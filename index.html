How this works

  1. Required drawdown (gal) = pump GPM × minimum run minutes. Aim for ≥1 minute on small pumps; 2 minutes is safer on larger pumps.
  2. We pick the smallest Amtrol Well-X-Trol size whose published drawdown at your switch setting meets that number (MC7025 card, Mar 2026).
  3. Set empty-tank air charge to cut-in minus 2 psi before connecting water.

Other brands differ. Always confirm against the tank’s own drawdown chart and a licensed well tech.
